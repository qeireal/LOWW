# Artist rig research and original LOWW tone recipes

Researched 28 September 2026. Sources describe particular products, tours or sessions; they do not establish one permanent setup for an artist. This document separates documented facts from proposed LOWW settings. No artist has authored, approved or endorsed these recipes. No proprietary preset, IR or recording was copied.

## What to build first

An original factory bank can capture useful characteristics of famous rigs now: solid clean lows, bright upper-mid drive, gritty amp blends and clean funk attack. LOWW does not model the named amplifiers, instruments, pickups or effects. These recipes are starting points, not measured reproductions of an artist's recorded tone. Audio matching and listening tests remain to be done.

Use descriptive preset names in the browser, with the inspiration and sources in documentation. A stronger future artist bank would involve commissioning bassists/producers to author presets and record a dry DI plus their approved reference sound.

## Where to find trustworthy data

1. **Manufacturer manuals for artist signature equipment.** Best for routing, frequency centres, parameter ranges and sometimes artist knob diagrams. Tech 21's Geddy manual and Darkglass's ADAM manual provide unusually useful descriptions. Product specifications describe the equipment, not necessarily settings used on an album.
2. **Original artist or touring-tech interviews.** Premier Guitar's Rig Rundown series interviews people who operate the rigs. Record the tour/date and distinguish the bassist's setup from guitarists appearing in the same article.
3. **Artist/producer demonstrations on their own or the manufacturer's channel.** Prefer a spoken explanation with isolated playing, legible settings, and an identified instrument. Store timestamps only after watching the relevant passage. Embedded videos were located here but no video timestamps or frame-read knob values are asserted.
4. **Original session walkthroughs.** Ask for the DI, printed bass stem, processing order, gain calibration and permission to use the material. A finished commercial mix alone cannot identify the bass processing uniquely.
5. **Community preset exchanges as leads.** Neural DSP hosts a [Parallax preset thread](https://unity.neuraldsp.com/t/parallax-preset-thread/34). User posts demonstrate what their authors shared; they are not evidence that a famous player used that setup. Follow attribution back to the author and confirm reuse terms before shipping anything.

Keep a record for each future recipe: artist, song/tour/year, source URL, speaker/author, observed settings, unknown settings, instrument/pickup/strings/tuning, signal order, cabinet/mic, input level, original LOWW mapping, listening notes, and asset permissions.

## Verified sources and what they support

### Geddy Lee — deep clean plus upper-range crunch

The [Tech 21 YYZ manual, pages 1 and 5–6](https://www.tech21nyc.com/t21manuals/Geddy_YYZ_OM.pdf) describes two parallel preamps and a blend of Deep and Drive paths. The Deep voicing is tailored to Geddy; the manual specifies low EQ at 80 Hz and high EQ at 3.2 kHz, with asymmetric mid boost/cut frequencies. Those are hardware specifications, not a published LOWW mapping or complete album setting.

**Inference for LOWW:** preserve a substantial clean low band while adding moderate mid drive and brighter high-band attack. A 1.5 kHz boost is only an approximation to the differently shaped Tech 21 mid control. LOWW's cabinet response is not the SansAmp speaker simulation.

### Chris Squire — blended bass and guitar amplification

The original [2013 Yes Rig Rundown](https://www.premierguitar.com/gear/rig-rundown-yes-steve-howe-and-chris-squire) documents Squire's 1964 Rickenbacker 4001, a vintage Marshall head blended with two Ampeg SVT-2PRO heads, and additional effects. His [2012 Squackett interview](https://www.premierguitar.com/artists/interview-steve-hackett-and-chris-squire-in-harmony-with-squackett) separately lists Marshall 4×12 and Ampeg 8×10 cabinets. These sources do not give a complete calibrated amp/mic blend or establish the recording chain for Roundabout.

**Inference for LOWW:** bright midrange drive over a firm low foundation. The new two-IR blend can express the cabinet concept better. Splitting one mono DI into frequency bands cannot recreate independent pickup outputs.

### Justin Chancellor — a useful effects source, limited base-tone evidence

The [official Dunlop signature wah page](https://www.jimdunlop.com/justin-chancellor-cry-baby-wah/) describes UK-filter, wah and fuzz modes, including clean reinforcement at 400 Hz during part of the sweep. It connects the filter voice to The Patient and the fuzz character to portions of Jambi. This documents a signature effect and his usage of it, not a full Tool amp chain or a transferable static EQ curve.

**Inference for LOWW:** a mid-present driven foundation can be useful before external modulation. Static LOWW EQ cannot replace the moving filter, wah or fuzz circuit. Do not label this recipe as a reproduction of either named song.

### Tim Commerford — document the era

The firsthand [2016 Wakrat interview](https://www.premierguitar.com/artists/bassists/tim-commerford-wakrat?page=1) distinguishes Lakland basses with black nylon flatwounds used on the record from Music Man instruments used live. It lists Ampeg amps and overdrive equipment. The official publisher's [2016 Rig Rundown video description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JsgUqLdgQ1U) identifies a 1978 SVT and PR-810H cabinet for the clean path. The [companion article](https://www.premierguitar.com/gear/rig-rundown-tim-commerford) identifies the dual-amp context.

**Inference for LOWW:** weighty clean lows with lower-mid growl and restrained top end. This is a Wakrat-era architectural inspiration, not an asserted Rage Against the Machine record setting.

### Adam “Nolly” Getgood — compressed clean sidechain and distortion

The [Darkglass ADAM manual, Signal Chain section](https://www.darkglass.com/pages/aggressively-distorting-advanced-machine-manual) documents distortion developed with Nolly, his captured cabinet IRs, and a fixed multiband compressor on the clean sidechain. Its Blend combines clean and processed paths; the clean path can be full-band or low-band depending on mode. The fixed multiband compressor's exact internal values are not published there.

**Inference for LOWW:** firm low compression, strong mid/high drive, reduced muddy mids and pronounced attack. LOWW's low compressor is not ADAM's hidden multiband compressor. This source supports the processing concept, not numerical duplication.

### Flea — clean foundation with separate special effects

The firsthand [2012 Rig Rundown with tech Tracy Robar](https://www.premierguitar.com/rig-rundown-red-hot-chili-peppers-flea) documents Modulus instruments, GK 2001RB amplifiers, 4×10/1×15 cabinets, and a separate boost/fuzz/filter/phaser board. It reports bass-instrument EQ with bass high and treble reduced. That is onboard EQ, not an instruction to apply identical plugin EQ. The [2017 follow-up](https://www.premierguitar.com/gear/rig-rundown-red-hot-chili-peppers-2017) is a useful separate tour reference; do not combine years into one supposed setup.

**Inference for LOWW:** mostly clean bands, modest low compression, enough upper range for percussive articulation. An envelope-filter song requires a separate filter effect. Slap technique and source instrument remain major variables.

### Lemmy — stronger amp evidence than exact plugin values

The manufacturer-authored [Marshall 1992LEM handbook](https://www.fullcompass.com/common/files/13867-LemmyAmplifierHeadBrochure.pdf), hosted by a dealer, describes the signature amp's relationship to his modified Super Bass, his 4×12/4×15 cabinets, and use of the bright channel's high-sensitivity input. Printed page 4 contains a preferred-settings diagram. The text was verified, but the diagram could not be rendered during this research, so no numerical knob transcription is claimed.

**Inference for LOWW:** strong mid drive, reduced sub weight, forward low/midrange and restrained fizz. LOWW lacks the amp's interacting tone stack and power-stage behaviour. A forum claim such as “bass and treble zero, mids ten” should not become a verified fact without checking the primary diagram or direct interview.

## Original starting presets

Every number below is an original proposed LOWW setting, inferred from the above concepts. None is an artist's documented knob value. These values are implemented as starting recipes in LOWW 0.2.0 and should be retuned after real-DI auditioning. Percentages are UI percentages; EQ and levels are dB; frequencies are Hz.

Implementation in 0.2.0: the seven rig studies use Parallel voicing with mono lows. The outer EQ values map to a 100 Hz low shelf and 5 kHz high shelf; the four middle values remain bell filters. These are deliberate LOWW interpretations of the research, not artist settings. Factory selections preserve the current cabinet mix; exported files start with stock A enabled and B disabled.

Common baseline: input 0 dB, output −9 dB, mix 100%, gate off, compressor/EQ/cabinet on, factory synthetic cabinet, all band levels as listed. Match perceived output loudness during auditioning; −9 dB is a starting value, not verified headroom for every DI. For the clean recipe, disable mid/high distortion rather than relying on a zero Drive setting to be perfectly linear.

## Preset and IR provenance

Keep source URLs and an `original LOWW approximation` description with these recipes. The public articles/manuals are evidence for independent tuning; they are not permission to redistribute an artist's recordings, commercial presets or cabinet IRs.

The [OwnHammer EULA](https://ownhammer.com/pages/eula) explicitly prohibits redistribution of its IR files. That matters because LOWW currently embeds an imported IR in saved presets: a preset file can therefore distribute audio data even when its extension looks like a settings file. For a distributable bank, use LOWW's synthetic stock response, independently captured IRs with documented rights, or assets explicitly licensed for redistribution. A free download does not by itself establish that permission. No rights to redistribute Neural DSP or Darkglass factory assets were established by this research.

For future external assets, record the author, original download page, exact license/version, attribution requirements and whether commercial redistribution in a plugin/preset is allowed. Ask the creator for explicit permission where terms are missing. This is a product asset policy, not a conclusion that every preset format has identical legal treatment.

For the verified Freesound candidates, preserve author/title/source, link [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), and identify edits such as trimming/resampling. Check each additional asset's creator page separately. No candidate WAVs were downloaded, auditioned or added to LOWW in this research.

**Implementation recommendation:** retain the real dual-IR level/pan/polarity controls now available. Add microphone selection or position controls only after obtaining a consistent measured bank. For a bass bank, commissioning captures is the clearest next step: one 4×10 or 8×10 cabinet, several microphones, labelled radial positions and distances, unchanged capture gain, reference timing and written redistribution rights. Record cabinet/speaker identity, mic angle and distance for every file. Validate phase alignment and interpolation audibly before describing intermediate settings as virtual mic positions; a darkening EQ over one IR is insufficient evidence of a measured microphone model.

## Validation before calling a tone matched

Record a consistent dry DI, instrument/pickup/string/tuning details and input calibration. Reamp the same performance through both chains; loudness-match before blind listening. Check sustained lows, picked transients, slap peaks, muted notes and chords. Compare dynamics and spectral balance, then audition in a mix. Log the final parameters, DSP version and cabinet checksum. Synthesized test signals establish stability and routing; they cannot establish artist-tone similarity.

The immediate bank can ship as original tone starting points after listening review. Artist names or song references should remain clearly attributed inspirations unless the relevant artist actually participates in creating and approving the presets.
